First Principles of
Nonduality

Strip away assumption until you hit bedrock. Five propositions that all traditions converge on.

Most nondual books either presuppose a tradition — you need to trust Ramana, or trust the Course — or presuppose the seeking. This project applies first-principles reasoning to the inner life. Not devotionally. Not academically. As direct inquiry.

The Five Principles

One. Awareness is prior to everything. Not a product of the brain — the ground in which all experience arises.

Two. Separation is not real — but it is believed. The felt sense of being a separate self is a perceptual distortion, not ontological fact. This is separation bias.

Three. What you seek, you already are. Not attainment. Recognition.

Four. The via negativa is not enough. Removing false belief clears the ground. But something must be recognized as that ground.

Five. Practice is recognition, not construction. You are not building anything. You are noticing what was never absent.
