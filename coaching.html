On Coaching

Relaxed, clear, and potent — a space to think clearly, lead with confidence, and meet complexity with calm precision.

I coach senior leaders in technology, creative, and nonprofit organizations — typically Sr. Directors through C-Suite. The work begins with practical leadership challenges: developing executive presence, managing conflict, strengthening influence, navigating transition.

Through direct conversation, leaders gain clarity on the patterns that constrain their effectiveness and discover more spacious ways of working. Whether you're navigating challenging relationships, facing leadership transitions, working through complex team dynamics, or exploring deeper questions of purpose — this approach blends traditional coaching techniques with decades of contemplative practice.

What I've witnessed again and again — both in my own life and in the lives of those I work with — is that seeing from the eyes of wholeness changes everything. It's not a luxury. It's the ground from which real clarity and transformation emerge. Often, what needs to change becomes obvious. What once seemed impossible becomes workable and even joyful.
