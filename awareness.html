Awareness Is Prior
to Everything

Not prior in time. Prior in nature. The ground in which all experience arises.

There is something happening right now that you are not causing.

You are reading these words. Sounds are arriving. Sensations move through the body. A thought arises about what this might mean, or whether it's true.

You did not produce any of this. It arrived.

And something received it.

That receiving — that open, effortless knowing — is what this page is about. Not what you know. Not the content. The knowing itself. The awareness in which this moment is presently occurring.

Awareness is not produced by the brain or body. It is the ground in which the brain, the body, and all their contents appear. This is the first and most fundamental of the five first principles: awareness is prior to everything that arises within it.

As explored in Unbroken Presence: you can forget to notice, but you can't fall outside noticing. The awareness that sees distraction was never distracted. The fog is known by what it seems to obscure.

This is not a claim about metaphysics. It is a pointer to direct experience. Right now, whatever is present is known. And there is something here that knows it — something that does not come and go with the content it knows.

The recognition of this is not an achievement. It is a noticing. And what is noticed has always been the case. As What Remains? points out: forgetting and remembering both occur in that knowing.
